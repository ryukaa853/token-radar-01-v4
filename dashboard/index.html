How the implied value is fitted

Each category's multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of the protocols in that category whose token already trades. A category with fewer than three trading comparables falls back to the whole-universe median; a universe with none falls back to 0.25. The multiple is clamped to 0.02–5. Then:

implied = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity

growth is 1 + the 7-day TVL change (clamped to −60%…+150%) × 0.5; maturity is 0.6 + up to 0.4 for up to three years tracked with no token (unknown listing date counts as one year).
